## 🏢 Who We Are

Henan GXON Agro Machinery Co., Ltd. (also trading as **GXON Agro Equipment Co., Ltd.**) is a manufacturer specializing in **grain drying and post-harvest processing equipment**. Headquartered in Henan Province — the heart of China's agricultural-machinery industry — we design, build, and export grain dryers to farms, cooperatives, and grain-trading companies across Asia, Africa, Latin America, and beyond.

Our mission is simple: **help every farm preserve more grain, waste less, and earn more** through durable, easy-to-operate drying technology.

## 🚜 What We Build

We focus on post-harvest grain handling. Our core product lines include:

| Product Line                       | Description                                                                                                    |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Batch Grain Dryers**             | Recirculating batch dryers for rice, wheat, corn, soybeans, and more — ideal for farms and small cooperatives. |
| **Continuous Grain Dryers**        | High-throughput drying towers for grain stations and commercial processors.                                    |
| **Heat-Pump / Low-Temp Dryers**    | Energy-efficient, gentle drying for seed and high-value grains.                                                |
| **Mixed-Flow & Cross-Flow Dryers** | Configurations matched to local climate, fuel availability, and grain type.                                    |
| **Auxiliary Equipment**            | Cleaning, conveying, and storage systems that integrate with drying lines.                                     |

Every machine is built for **fuel flexibility** (rice husk, biomass, coal, diesel, gas, or electricity) and **remote-monitoring readiness**, so operators can manage a drying cycle from a phone.
